# Elephant Healthcare

> Global health-technology company partnering with governments to digitise and strengthen public health systems across Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia. Flagship product ElephantOS is a mobile- and offline-first EHR serving 19M+ contracted patients in Nigeria, Kenya and Pakistan. No public developer API is published today; Elephant Platform is an emerging AI-driven marketplace for third-party clinical integrations.

## Products
- [ElephantOS](https://www.elephant.healthcare/digitise): Mobile- and offline-first electronic health record for primary/secondary care facilities.
- [Elephant Nudge](https://www.elephant.healthcare/product): Clinical co-pilot / decision-support that guides and rewards quality of care (launched 2024).
- [Elephant Platform](https://www.elephant.healthcare/integrate): AI-powered marketplace connecting clinicians to third-party software, medical devices, and supply-chain partners (limited availability).
